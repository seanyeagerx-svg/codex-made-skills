# Storyboard Screening

## Keep Two Pools Separate

- External references: real sequences or shots that demonstrate useful staging, transitions, or performance. Cite the work and inspected span.
- Original alternatives: newly proposed ways to stage the user's scene. They are design proposals, not found examples or validated video outputs.

Clarify the candidate unit: shot reference, complete sequence plan, scene treatment, or storytelling mechanism. Do not turn a 30-second story into 100 shots to satisfy a research quota.

## Start From Story Invariants

Capture the user-approved event order, character goals, emotional turn, required actions, runtime, setting, and prohibitions. Keep unresolved choices visible. Proposing a plot change does not replace the user's plot. For missing details, use minimal reversible assumptions rather than expanding the story.

## Minimum Criteria

| Dimension | Inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Story clarity | Can the viewer understand the essential action and cause/effect? |
| Emotional progression | Does staging/performance support the intended turn rather than decorative coverage? |
| Spatial continuity | Blocking, screen direction, eyelines, props, and transitions across shots |
| Timing | Actions, reaction holds, travel and cuts plausibly fit the requested duration |
| Production feasibility | Asset consistency, interaction complexity, camera/action conflicts, required effects |

Evaluate the sequence as a whole. Independently impressive shots can create an incoherent edit. For proposed timelines, label durations as estimates, check sums/gaps/overlaps, and preserve time for emotional processing, not just physical actions.

Do not claim a platform requires exact timestamps, supports a model, or can generate a stated duration without current primary-source verification. A designed timing plan is neither a recovered source timecode nor a guarantee of generated edit timing.

## Deliverable

Compare a manageable number of genuinely different full approaches if ideation was requested. Include the sequence/beat coverage, key difference, tradeoff, timing status, and unresolved feasibility. For reference research, show the actual evidence and which mechanism transfers, not unauthorized wholesale reproduction of a script.

Do not mix all winning shots into a new master plan without rechecking continuity and obtaining agreement on material changes. Later prompt generation can use an available specialist skill, but is not required for screening and must preserve this handoff's confirmed versus proposed distinction.
