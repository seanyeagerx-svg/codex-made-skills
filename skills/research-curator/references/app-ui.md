# Application UI Screening

## Unit and Source Strategy

Identify the audience, task, device/input context, and candidate unit: complete task flow, individual screen, component pattern, or visual direction. A marketing landing page is not a substitute for an operational application.

Prefer live products, public demos, official design systems, and documented flows when interaction matters. Galleries can find visual directions, but their thumbnails cannot establish usability. No private account access, purchase, form submission, or change to real user data merely to inspect a reference.

## Minimum Criteria

| Dimension | Inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Task fit | Does the pattern solve this audience's actual repeated task? |
| Information hierarchy | Scanability, density, grouping, clarity of actions and status |
| Interaction completeness | Entry, navigation/back, loading, empty, error, success and recovery as relevant |
| Accessibility and responsiveness | Available keyboard/focus/contrast evidence, target size, narrow/wide layouts |
| Transferability | Components, implementation complexity, content/assets, licensing and ongoing maintenance |

Do not claim accessibility compliance from appearance. Indicate which interactions and viewports were actually checked. Screenshot-only candidates can qualify for a visual-only brief; they remain provisional for unobserved functional requirements.

## Compare and Deliver

- Extract reusable mechanisms such as filtering, multi-step progress, dense comparison, or error recovery, rather than "looks premium".
- Show real observed screens/states and direct links. Include what was not tested.
- Rank fit for the target workflow separately from visual appeal. Strong hero animation may be irrelevant to a frequently used data-entry tool.
- State asset and implementation dependencies, known reuse terms, and uncertainty. Inspiration does not grant a license to copy brand art or proprietary code.
- A recommended next step may be a small mockup or interaction test. Do not build, install, publish, or operate real forms until that task is requested.
