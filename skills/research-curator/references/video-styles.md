# Video Style Screening

## Unit and Sources

Choose whether the user needs complete films, excerpts, visual style families, camera treatments, or production methods. Count distinct works separately from families and repeated excerpts. A film trailer, a still, and a behind-the-scenes page can support different claims about one work, not three independent successes.

Use original filmmaker/artist releases, production breakdowns, and inspectable model/platform showcases. Collections and social feeds are discovery routes, not provenance. Verify current access and provenance on a small sample. A work that looks AI-generated is not evidence it was made with AI, much less with a specific model.

## Minimum Criteria

| Dimension | Inspect | Limitation to retain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visual system | Color, light, texture, framing, subject hierarchy across the clip | One strong still may hide inconsistency |
| Motion and editing | Subject motion, camera behavior, cut rhythm, continuity | Requires temporally inspectable footage |
| Narrative fit | Emotional beat, subject, pacing, and intended audience | Impressive effects may defeat a calm brief |
| Transferability | Needed assets, shot complexity, known process and budget | Unknown workflow stays unknown |
| Execution stability | Visible identity, geometry, contact, physics and transitions | Only judge the inspected span; no success-rate claim |

Hard gates come from the task: permitted aesthetic, runtime, topic, required medium, or available production resources. Do not impose a universal shot length or forbid deliberate stylization.

## Evidence and Output

- Record exact viewed intervals and whether audio was available. Audio judgments require listening.
- Motion and cut timing need playback or sufficient ordered temporal evidence; isolated frames establish only static observations. State sampling limitations, never "watched" when only frames were sampled.
- Distinguish visual inspiration, creator-reported workflow, independently observed process, and an actually reproduced result.
- For an AI-only brief, unverified AI attribution is provisional, not a confirmed finalist. For a general visual brief, live-action references may be useful if labeled accurately.
- Show preview/link, clip locator, borrowable style mechanism, constraints, and main uncertainty for each finalist. Do not claim a generator can reproduce it simply because it can be described.
- A next step can be a small authorized motion test; selecting the reference does not authorize running it.
