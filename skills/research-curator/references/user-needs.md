# Need Screening

## Select the Subtype

- Single-client brief: clarify mandatory, negotiable, conflicting, and unknown requirements. Do not remove mandatory items just to produce a top seven.
- Market/product discovery: cluster authorized feedback and prioritize problems to investigate. Do not invent 100 needs or collect private conversations to fill a quota.

Count raw records, meaningfully distinct needs, and independent contributors separately. One person repeating a complaint is not multiple customers. Anonymous records with unclear duplication do not justify a precise user count.

## Evidence Record

For each need, capture anonymized source ID, affected user/context, the problem in their words or a faithful short paraphrase, observed/reported consequence, current workaround, frequency if known, and unresolved assumptions.

Separate the underlying problem from a requested solution. "Add a button" may describe a wish; the job and obstacle need evidence. AI-inferred needs are hypotheses, never user quotes. Public research may complement authorized inputs only when within scope, and must remain separately sourced.

## Minimum Criteria

| Dimension | Inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence strength | Independent, relevant sources and concrete examples, not repeated language |
| Impact and urgency | Consequence, severity, blocking effect, time sensitivity |
| Reach | Known affected users/contexts; unknown denominators stay unknown |
| Goal fit | Alignment with the client's or product's declared objective |
| Feasibility and cost | Dependencies, effort, risks, and confidence of estimates |

Frequency is not importance. A rare access or safety blocker may outrank a popular cosmetic wish. Stated preference is not willingness to pay; complaints alone are not market demand validation. Estimates are estimates, not measured outcomes.

## Output

For a single client: show mandatory items intact, optional priorities, conflicts, and the few questions needed. For a market: show prioritized problems with evidence, counterevidence, uncertainties, and the next validation step.

Trace every merged cluster to source IDs and retain distinct constraints within it. Do not erase disagreements by averaging them. Label requirements as confirmed only if actually confirmed. No contacting customers, modifying a CRM, or using private records outside the authorized pool.
